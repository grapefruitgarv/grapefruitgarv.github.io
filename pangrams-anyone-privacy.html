Privacy Policy — Pangrams Anyone?

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Developer: David Garver
Contact: garver.apps@gmail.com

Summary

Pangrams Anyone? does not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Pangrams Anyone? has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not receive your name, email address, location, device identifier, or gameplay data.

What is stored on your device

Your game data — puzzles, found words, scores, streaks, badges, saved games, custom words, and settings such as theme, minimum word length, and letter reuse — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

If you have iCloud device backup turned on in iOS Settings, your device backup may include this local app data, under Apple's terms and your own iCloud settings. We do not access that backup.

Third parties

There are no third-party services in the app. The app makes no network requests to us or to any analytics or advertising company.

Downloading the app and any purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple may collect information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID information.

Children

Pangrams Anyone? is a general-audience word game. Because we collect no personal data, we knowingly collect no personal data from children either.

Your choices

There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected. You can delete all of your game data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if online play, accounts, or analytics are added — this policy will be updated, and the effective date above will change.
