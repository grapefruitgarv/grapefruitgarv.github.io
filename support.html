App Support

Support for apps by David Garver: Pangrams Anyone? and Track My Pottery.

Contact

Email: garver.apps@gmail.com

Please include which app you are writing about, what happened, and what you expected to happen. If it is a bug, the steps to reproduce it and your device model and iOS version help. If you are testing a beta version through TestFlight, please say so and include the app version. If you want to provide feedback, that's welcome as well.

Pangrams Anyone?

A word game. Questions about gameplay, word validity, scores, streaks, badges, and saved games are welcome at the email above.

Track My Pottery

A studio tool for tracking pottery pieces. Questions about pieces, firing logs, cost estimates, photos, and saving buyers to contacts are welcome at the email above.

Accounts

Neither app has accounts or sign-in, so there is nothing to log into and no password to recover. All of your data stays on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.
