Privacy Policy — Track My Pottery

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Developer: David Garver
Contact: garver.apps@gmail.com

Summary

Track My Pottery does not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Track My Pottery has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not receive your name, email address, location, device identifier, or pottery data. The app makes no network requests to us.

What is stored on your device

Everything you enter — pieces, stages, finishes, clay bodies, firing logs, time logs, cost estimates, sales records, and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is deleted when you delete the app.

If you have iCloud device backup turned on in iOS Settings, your device backup may include this local app data, under Apple's terms and your own iCloud settings. We do not access that backup.

Photos

If you grant access, the app can take a photo with your camera or choose a photo from your library, to attach to a pottery piece. Those photos are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded or shared by the app. You can revoke photo and camera access at any time in iOS Settings > Track My Pottery.

Contacts

Only if you choose to save a buyer: the app can read the contact you pick, and save that buyer to the “Ceramics Customers” group in your device's Contacts app. That happens only on your device, only when you tap to save a buyer, and the contact data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can revoke contacts access at any time in iOS Settings > Track My Pottery.

Third parties

There are no third-party services in the app. Downloading the app and any purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple may collect information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID information.

Children

Track My Pottery is a general-audience studio tool. Because we collect no personal data, we knowingly collect no personal data from children either.

Your choices

There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected. You can delete all of your app data at any time by deleting the app from your device. Contacts you chose to save remain in your device's Contacts app until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if cloud sync, accounts, or analytics are added — this policy will be updated, and the effective date above will change.
